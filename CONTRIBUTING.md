# Contributing to mBox 2 Resurrection

Thank you for your interest in the project. Useful contributions are welcome, including research, documentation, testing and software development.

## Before contributing

- Read the [project goals](docs/project-goals.md) and [development roadmap](docs/development-roadmap.md).
- Check existing issues and discussions (if enabled) to avoid duplicating work.
- Keep the initial hardware target in mind: the original full-size Digidesign Mbox 2.
- Be clear about what you personally observed and what you are inferring.

## Research contributions

Research is especially valuable at this stage. When sharing a finding, include as much of the following as is practical:

- The operating system and version.
- The exact hardware model and revision, if known.
- Driver, firmware or utility version.
- The commands, tools or procedure used.
- Relevant output, logs, screenshots or disassembly.
- The result and what it does — and does not — demonstrate.
- Links to original sources where possible.

Please distinguish direct observations from interpretations. Use the status labels in the development roadmap: Confirmed, Strong indication, Hypothesis, Open question or Not tested.

Do not publish copyrighted driver binaries, firmware or documentation unless you have permission to do so. Small excerpts, identifiers and independently produced analysis may still have legal considerations; when in doubt, link to the original source rather than uploading it.

## Code and pull requests

- Keep changes focused and explain the problem they solve.
- Include the reasoning and evidence behind hardware-specific behaviour.
- Avoid claiming support for a feature until it has been tested.
- Document operating-system, hardware and build requirements.
- Do not include proprietary files, credentials, personal information or unrelated generated files.
- If a change affects a working legacy setup, explain the impact and provide a way to validate or reverse it.

A pull request should describe:
1. What changed.
2. Why it changed.
3. How it was tested (or state that it was not tested).
4. Any limitations or unanswered questions.

## Questions and uncertain findings

It is fine to submit an incomplete finding or ask a question. Mark uncertainty clearly; an honest open question is more useful than an unsupported conclusion.

You must have the right to submit the material. These project licences do not change the licence of third-party material included or referenced in a contribution.

## Scope

The original full-size Mbox 2 is the priority. Research into other Mbox models or other audio interfaces can be useful, but should be clearly separated from claims about the target device.
